Sample Import Packet

TOSSUPS

1. This 1969 event saw Neil Armstrong become the first person to walk on this celestial body, landing there aboard the Eagle module.
ANSWER: MOON

2. This element with atomic number 79 and symbol Au is prized for its conductivity and resistance to tarnish.
ANSWER: GOLD

3. This programming language, created by Guido van Rossum and first released in 1991, is named after a British comedy troupe.
ANSWER: PYTHON

BONUSES

1. For 10 points each, name these capital cities of European countries.
[10] This city on the Seine is the capital of France.
ANSWER: PARIS
[10] This city on the Tiber is the capital of Italy.
ANSWER: ROME
[10] This city on the Spree is the capital of Germany.
ANSWER: BERLIN

2. For 10 points each, answer the following about basic arithmetic.
[10] This is the sum of two and two.
ANSWER: FOUR
[10] This is the product of three and three.
ANSWER: NINE
[10] This is the square root of sixteen.
ANSWER: FOUR
